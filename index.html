Methodology, basis, sources & risks

Derivation (workload from the book). Distinct apps = apps/customer × (overlap + customers × (1 − overlap)) — the shared core is counted once, each customer adds only their unique line-of-business apps. Packaging requests/mo = distinct apps × monthly repackage rate. Patch & update ops = distinct apps × per-app minutes/mo. App tickets = seats × tickets per 100 seats. Staffing follows workload, not user population (MetricNet) — which is why FTE scales with the book, not the rate.

Cost method. Packaging labor = requests × per-app hours (standard/custom mix) × environments per app; the platform sets the environment factor to 1 (one definition serves all targets) at lower per-app hours. Patching and ticket labor are reduced by the stated percentages. Each labor line = hours × fully-loaded cost; the platform column adds the license. Hours and FTE are rate-independent; only dollars scale with the labor rate. Value-driver attribution uses Shapley values — each capability credited with its average marginal contribution, summing exactly to the total.

  1. App portfolios. Okta Businesses at Work 2025: average 101 apps per organization; large companies (≥2,000 employees) ~211; newest/smallest ~22 — basis for the apps-per-customer segment hints.
  2. Packaging effort. Camwood: packaging consumes 30–40% of IT-admin time at 60–70% first-pass success. Recast research: 4+ hrs per app; even small orgs repackage 5–10 apps/mo (≈10% of portfolio); mid-to-large estates 100–300+/mo. Community benchmarks: complex apps 8–40 hrs. Estates commonly maintain 2–4 package formats.
  3. Ticket volume. Software & applications is the largest help-desk category — over a third of all volume (Fixify 2026 benchmark); desk volume typically ~0.5–1+ tickets/user/mo (MetricNet). Default of 6 app install/request tickets per 100 seats is deliberately conservative. Self-service deflects 50–70% of software-request tickets (Giva); enterprise tier-1 median ≈41%.
  4. Labor cost. 2026 rate guides: US fully-loaded $80–150/hr; India offshore $20–45/hr; blended teams $30–60/hr — selector uses $95/$65/$40 mid-points.
  5. MSP economics. Recurring-services gross margin 50–60%; labor 90%+ of service COGS (Service Leadership / ConnectWise).
  6. Platform. Application Workspace complements Intune, ConfigMgr, Citrix, AVD, Windows 365 — no rip-and-replace; managed, tested catalog (8,000+ packages) updated daily; self-service; agent install during Autopilot (Recast).
  • Excludes one-time onboarding, migration and enablement; benefit ramps as adoption completes.
  • License rate is partner-tier dependent and must be confirmed before finalizing.
  • Largest sensitivities: apps per customer, overlap, environments/app, and residual custom-packaging effort — all editable, all shown live in Derived workload.
  • Dollar figures depend on the loaded labor rate; hours and FTE do not.
  • Planning model, not a substitute for a measured baseline or proof-of-value.

AWhere the savings come from

These are the ways Application Workspace lifts partner profitability. Each removes a specific block of labor your team performs today; the catalog runs 8,000+ apps for you, one agent deploys to every device, and multi-tenancy (Section B) serves every customer from a single build. Contributions are Shapley-credited and sum exactly to the total labor reduction — adjust any assumption at left to see the mix change.

① Recast runs 8,000+ apps end-to-end
—
Recast packages, tests, deploys, updates, patches and CVE-scans 8,000+ standard applications and maintains them daily. Your engineers never touch this estate — the entire off-the-shelf lifecycle leaves your plate, not just the initial build. Value = standard-app build and maintenance effort avoided.
② One agent auto-deploys to every device
—
A single agent delivers every app to Windows, macOS, VDI, Cloud PC and BYOD across Intune, Configuration Manager, Citrix, AVD and Jamf — from one definition, with no per-format rebuild. Value = the per-environment rebuild and manual deployment effort eliminated.
③ Your custom apps, automated
—
For the applications that are genuinely your own, platform automation cuts build effort per app; the team keeps only what is truly bespoke. Value = reduced hours on custom builds.
④ Self-service & identity-based access
—
Users pull approved applications from a role-based catalog, so fewer install and request tickets reach the service desk. Value = support hours deflected.
⑤ Staged patch pipeline
—
Updates roll to every environment through one staged pipeline with rollback — no per-platform rebuilds. Value = patch validation and deployment hours reduced.
